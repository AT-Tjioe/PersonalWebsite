Featured Firearms

AK-47

The AK-47 is a legendary, gas-operated rifle chambered for the 7.62×39mm intermediate cartridge. Developed by Mikhail Kalashnikov, it has become one of the most widely recognized and durable firearms globally.

M4A1

A selective-fire, gas-operated rifle chambered for the 5.56×45mm NATO cartridge. Widely utilized by the United States military, it is renowned for its lightweight modularity and tactical reliability.

Desert Eagle

The Desert Eagle is an iconic, large-caliber semi-automatic pistol. Universally celebrated for its distinct visual profile, high stopping power, and unique gas-operated mechanism.

Glock 19

A compact, highly reliable semi-automatic pistol chambered in 9x19mm. Known for its simplicity and tactical durability.

Remington 870

A classic pump-action shotgun widely recognized for its robust construction, smooth cycle actions, and versatile reliability in sports shooting.
