Top Rated Guns

AK-47

The AK-47, officially known as the Avtomat Kalashnikova, is a Soviet-designed, gas-operated assault rifle chambered for the 7.62×39mm intermediate cartridge. Developed by Mikhail Kalashnikov in 1947 and officially adopted by the Soviet military in 1949, it has become the most widely produced and influential firearm in history, with an estimated 75 to 100 million units built globally.

M4A1

The M4A1 is a selective-fire, gas-operated assault rifle chambered for the 5.56×45mm NATO cartridge. It is a further development of the M16 rifle and is widely used by the United States military and other allied forces.

Desert Eagle

The Desert Eagle is a large-caliber semi-automatic pistol designed by John Garand and manufactured by Eagle Industries.
