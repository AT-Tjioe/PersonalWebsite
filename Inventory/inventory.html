Available Firearms

AK-47

The AK-47 is a legendary, gas-operated rifle chambered for the 7.62×39mm intermediate cartridge. Developed by Mikhail Kalashnikov, it is known worldwide for its reliability in extreme conditions.

M4A1

A selective-fire, gas-operated rifle chambered for the 5.56×45mm NATO cartridge. Widely utilized by the United States military, it is renowned for its lightweight modularity.

Desert Eagle

The Desert Eagle is an iconic, large-caliber semi-automatic pistol. Universally celebrated for its distinct visual profile, high stopping power, and unique gas-operated system.

Glock 19

A compact, highly reliable semi-automatic pistol chambered in 9x19mm. It serves as a global gold standard for personal defense, security forces, and law enforcement.

Remington 870

A classic pump-action shotgun widely recognized for its robust steel receiver, smooth cycle actions, and versatile reliability in tactical environments or sports shooting.
